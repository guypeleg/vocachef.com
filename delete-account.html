Delete Your Account

Last updated: March 2026

You can delete your VocaChef account at any time directly from within the app. Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

⚠️ Warning: Deleting your account will permanently remove all your data, including your profile, session history, and subscription status. This action cannot be reversed.

How to delete your account

  1. 1
    Open the VocaChef app on your Android device.
  2. 2
    Tap the Settings icon (⚙️) at the top of the home screen.
  3. 3
    Scroll down to the My Data section.
  4. 4
    Tap Delete my account and confirm when prompted.

Your account and all associated data will be deleted immediately from our systems.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

✓  Your profile (name, email address)
✓  Your session and usage history
✓  Your subscription status
✓  All app preferences and settings

Note: If you have an active Pro subscription, deleting your account does not automatically cancel your Google Play subscription. Please cancel it separately through Google Play → Subscriptions before or after deleting your account.

Need help?

If you're having trouble deleting your account from the app, or if you'd like to request manual deletion, please contact us at support@vocachef.com and we'll take care of it within 30 days.
